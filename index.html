    grouping and hierarchy

    The flows stay the same. Two key screens inside BookMyShow are regrouped so related things sit together, with one clear headline and one main action.

    01

    Checkout: the split widget

    • GroupingSplit payment becomes one bright card in the checkout, with the invite list inside it, so everything about friends sits in one place.
    • HierarchyA title and a one-line promise lead: “Let your friends pre-pay in real time”. Each contact is a name over a number, with one Add button.
    • ActionTap to open, search, tap Add. A tick replaces Add, so it is clear who is in.
